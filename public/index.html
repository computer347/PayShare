PayShare

Pay as a group with PayPal. Nobody is charged until everyone is in, and you see who has actually paid.

Before you buy

Booking a cabin with friends? Everyone puts a hold on their share in PayPal. When the last one lands, the booking goes through. If someone doesn't make it in 15 minutes, every hold is released.

🏡 Try the group checkout

After the bill

Snap a receipt: AI reads the items, everyone taps what they had and pays their part. PayShare forwards it to whoever paid the bill.
